4. RULES
- Only U-Bahn stations are included; S-Bahn and mainline stations are not.
- Closed U-Bahn stations are included, because BVG's plan archive also contains plans of them.
- A former name is only added if a source confirms it.
- Some former names are common words (Hauptstraße, Tegel, Kreuzberg, Flughafen, Stadion, Knie)
  and may also appear on plans as street or district names.
